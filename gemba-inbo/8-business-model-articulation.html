Better screening is a lever in its own right.

  1. Use more independent evaluations. Aggregate judgements (BIN); the crowd beats the individual.
  2. Anchor on purchase intent. Base evaluation on would-you-buy, not would-you-like.
  3. Screen in stages. Multiple, cheaper-first passes before expensive commitment.
